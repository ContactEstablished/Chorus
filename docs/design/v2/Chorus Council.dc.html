Synthesis — Council Review screen, 12 questions
RULING · Q3 PHASE INDICATOR
All three deliberators converged on making the phase track the primary progress affordance, and two of the three independently rejected a percentage bar. I rule with them: the run has five named phases and a round counter, both of which are knowable, whereas remaining time is not. Position B's re-entry test — "where is it now, answered in under a second" — is the right acceptance criterion, and it implies the current phase must be legible without reading the transcript at all.
Position A dissented on motion, arguing that a per-member spinner competes with the phase track for the same attention. This is consistent with the app's stated thesis that attention is the scarce resource, so the dissent is upheld in part: per-member state stays a stable marker, and the only moving element during a round is the active phase segment.
RULING · Q7 UNAVAILABLE MEMBERS
Unanimous, and I see no reason to complicate it: an unavailable or refusing member keeps its roster card and gains a red explanation line, and its refused turns appear in the transcript as rows rather than as absences. A council that quietly shrinks cannot be audited afterwards, and the refusal-to-run behaviour becomes unreadable if the roster hides the member that caused it.
UNRESOLVED · Q11 TRANSCRIPT RETENTION
Two deliberators asked for the transcript to be retained per run and diffable across runs; the third argued it is a debugging artifact and should be dropped. I decline to rule — this is a product decision about storage the council has no information about, and the brief does not ask for it. Flagged for the author rather than answered.
